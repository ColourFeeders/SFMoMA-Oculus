Oculus Mac Host Monitor v0.2.6

The offline suite uses local signed UDP and loopback HTTPS to verify
authentication, the reset pre-send check, the 60-second contact-loss gate,
five-minute retries, persisted attempt spacing and planned-restart holds.
UPS policy tests use simulated readings and clock advancement for the
ten-minute battery and three-minute AC thresholds.
The tests do not send traffic to exhibit hosts.

Verify launchd installation, Restart Guard startup, the actual PiKVM
ATX reset and Windows recovery on the installed equipment.
